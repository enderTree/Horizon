## [Ant Lingbo Open-Sources LingBot-Video: First MoE Embodied Video Model](https://www.qbitai.com/2026/07/446458.html) ⭐️ 9.0/10

This marks a significant breakthrough in embodied AI by combining MoE efficiency with video generation, enabling faster and more scalable robot skill learning. The open-source release under Apache 2.0 will accelerate research in robotics simulation, action prediction, and world models. LingBot-Video has 30 billion total parameters but only activates about 3 billion per inference, achieving roughly three times the inference efficiency of a comparable dense architecture. It uses a DiT+MoE design, trains on 70,000 hours of embodied data, and incorporates multi-dimensional reinforcement learning rewards focusing on physical plausibility and task completion.

Diffusion Transformer (DiT) replaces the traditional U-Net in diffusion models with a transformer backbone for scalable video generation. RBench is a new benchmark tailored for evaluating robot-oriented video generation across tasks like manipulation and mobile navigation. LingBot-Video combines these techniques to generate videos that are physically consistent and task-aware, bridging simulation and real-world robot learning.

## [IMGNet: Face Verification with Sign Patterns Instead of Cosine Similarity](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1urxvxh/i_built_imgnet_a_face_verification_model_that/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

IMGNet introduces a face verification model that replaces cosine similarity with sliding window sign pattern matching, achieving 96.27% on LFW with a model size of only 10.58 MB. This novel similarity metric challenges the conventional face verification paradigm and could enable more efficient and robust lightweight models, potentially reducing computational costs while maintaining competitive accuracy. The model was trained on CASIA-WebFace (490k images) and includes a SW Block that computes pixel-wise differences at prime window sizes, an IMG Sign MSE Loss that only depends on sign agreement, and a voting system (2/3 or 3/3 for match). When applied to ArcFace embeddings without retraining, IMG Sign Score achieves 99.58% on LFW, only 0.24% below ArcFace+Cosine.

reddit · r/MachineLearning · /u/img-_- · Jul 9, 18:00

**Background**: Face verification typically encodes face images into embedding vectors and compares them using cosine similarity. IMGNet's SW Block replaces standard convolution with a multi-scale relational operation that computes differences between each pixel and its neighbors at prime window sizes (3, 5, 7), then maps these differences through a small MLP. The loss function is defined purely over sign pattern agreement, eliminating amplitude dependency.
