## [Generative Design of Viable Bacteriophage Genomes via Genome Language Models](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1vjj4pr/r_generative_design_of_novel_bacteriophages_with/) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Researchers used the genome language models Evo 1 and Evo 2 to generate whole-genome sequences for bacteriophages, using ΦX174 as a template, and experimentally confirmed 16 of the designed phages were viable. This marks the first generative design of viable bacteriophage genomes. This work demonstrates that genome language models can generate functional sequences at whole-genome scale, a major step toward AI-driven synthetic biology. It could accelerate the design of custom phages for applications such as phage therapy and microbiome engineering. The generated whole genomes exhibited realistic genetic architectures and desired host tropism, with 16 viable phages showing substantial evolutionary novelty. Evo 2, the newer model, is an open-source genomic foundation model trained on prokaryotic and eukaryotic DNA at single-nucleotide resolution.

reddit · r/MachineLearning · /u/moschles · Aug 9, 07:11

**Background**: Genome language models (gLMs) treat DNA and RNA sequences as biological text, using transformer architectures to learn genomic grammar and regulatory patterns from raw sequences. Evo 1 and Evo 2 are open-source foundation models developed by the Arc Institute and the University of California, trained directly on raw DNA sequences rather than natural language. Bacteriophages are viruses that infect bacteria; designing novel phages is valuable for phage therapy and for understanding viral evolution.

## [1998 W3C Essay on Cool URIs Rekindles Link Rot Debate](https://www.w3.org/Provider/Style/URI) ⭐️ 8.0/10

The classic 1998 W3C essay 'Cool URIs Don't Change' resurfaced on Hacker News, where a 43-comment thread debated its continued relevance. The discussion highlighted modern examples of link rot, including a broken NSF.gov link and Microsoft's failing support URLs. The thread underscores that link rot remains a pervasive problem decades after Berners-Lee's advice, affecting even major institutions. It reopens the conversation about persistent identifiers, redirects, and how SEO practices have shaped URL management. The W3C page has remained at the same URI for over 26 years, proving its own point about stability. Commenters noted the article predates widespread 301/302 redirects, and that modern CMSes like WordPress auto-redirect renamed slugs, partially mitigating but not solving the problem.

hackernews · Klaster_1 · Aug 9, 14:32 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49231809)

**Background**: Link rot refers to hyperlinks gradually breaking because the target page moves or disappears. Tim Berners-Lee wrote 'Cool URIs Don't Change' in 1998 to urge webmasters to design URLs that remain stable, warning that 'URIs don't change: people change them.' The essay has become a canonical piece of web architecture advice.

The piece quickly drew wide engagement, with 171 comments and substantial discussion on Hacker News. This matters because it underscores the escalating tension between ubiquitous AI surveillance and personal privacy in everyday life. The debate affects consumers, tech companies, and policy makers, raising urgent questions about corporate power and the need for counter-surveillance measures. These techniques illustrate the ongoing arms race between surveillance and privacy protection.

## [World's Largest AI Computing Facility Goes Live in Inner Mongolia](https://www.globaltimes.cn/page/202608/1367666.shtml) ⭐️ 8.0/10

On August 6, 2026, Envision Group announced that its 'Envision Ulanqab Galaxy Base' has officially entered operation. The base is the world's largest single AI computing facility, with a building area of 120,000 square meters and a planned total capacity of 2GW, supporting million-GPU parallel computing with over 80% green energy. This landmark infrastructure milestone gives China a major boost in AI compute capacity, as the base is the strongest single AI data center globally in terms of token output. It also demonstrates the feasibility of large-scale, green-powered AI clusters and reinforces the national 'East-Data-West-Computing' strategy. Located in Ulanqab, one of the eight national computing hub nodes, the base sits about 240 km from Beijing with a data transmission latency of only 4.2 milliseconds and electricity prices roughly 50% lower than the Beijing-Tianjin-Hebei region. It is the first flagship project of Envision's 'Gobi Mission' program, which aims to provide a replicable construction model for domestic AI computing clusters.

telegram · zaihuapd · Aug 9, 05:06

**Background**: China's 'East-Data-West-Computing' project, launched in 2022, routes data from the data-intensive eastern regions to western areas where renewable energy and land are more abundant, optimizing resource allocation. Ulanqab is a key node in this initiative, and companies such as Huawei, Alibaba, Apple, and Kuaishou have already deployed computing infrastructure there. The new Galaxy Base is specifically oriented toward AI workloads, using over 80% green electricity to reduce the carbon footprint of large-scale AI training and inference.
