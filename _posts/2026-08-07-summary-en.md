## [Chinese-Led BESIII Collaboration Confirms Existence of Glueballs](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/pvyNR1lN7QPx3IrpB3WtUg) ⭐️ 9.0/10

On August 6, the BESIII collaboration, led by Chinese scientists, announced the first experimental confirmation of glueballs, a new form of matter, after 15 years of research. The particle X(2370) was identified as dominated by a pseudoscalar glueball, matching lattice QCD predictions. This is a breakthrough in particle physics: glueballs were predicted by the Standard Model but had never been observed experimentally. The finding validates a key prediction about the strong force and marks a significant step toward understanding quantum chromodynamics. The team analyzed data from the BESIII detector at the Beijing Electron-Positron Collider II, including a sample of 10 billion J/ψ decays. X(2370) was first discovered in 2011; its quantum numbers were measured as 0⁻⁺ in 2024, and further studies confirmed its flavor-singlet nature, indicating a glueball-dominated state.

telegram · zaihuapd · Aug 6, 07:31

**Background**: Glueballs are hypothetical particles composed entirely of gluons, the carriers of the strong force, with no valence quarks. Because gluons carry color charge, they can interact with each other and form bound states. The BESIII experiment, located at the Beijing Electron-Positron Collider II, is designed to study hadron physics and τ-charm physics, and its large J/ψ sample enabled this search. The X(2370) particle's mass and quantum numbers are consistent with lattice QCD predictions for the lightest pseudoscalar glueball.

## [Bidirectional Diffusion Model Predicts Its Own Rollout Errors via Round-Trip Consistency](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1vh2gn1/roundtrip_consistency_bidirectional_diffusion/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Researchers trained a single bidirectional conditional latent diffusion model that steps dynamical systems forward or backward in time using a direction flag, and showed that round-trip discrepancy acts as a test-time error signal. The model outperforms direction-specific specialist models in both forward and backward generation. This provides a measurement-free, self-supervised way to estimate rollout error in generative models of dynamical systems, which is crucial for long-horizon applications such as video generation and digital twins. It could reduce reliance on ensembles, held-out data, or governing equations during deployment. The method requires no ensembles, held-out data, or governing equations; a single extra rollout in the reverse direction is sufficient. The paper is available at arXiv:2608.00675, with code and a project page provided.

reddit · r/MachineLearning · /u/Clean-Hovercraft5825 · Aug 6, 12:10

**Background**: Autoregressive generative models, such as latent diffusion or flow models, accumulate errors during long rollouts because each step is conditioned on previously generated, imperfect outputs. At deployment, there is usually no ground truth to measure this drift. Round-trip consistency trains one model to generate both forward and backward in time; if a forward rollout followed by a backward rollout fails to return to the starting point, the discrepancy is a cheap proxy for the hidden rollout error.
